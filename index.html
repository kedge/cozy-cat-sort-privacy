English简体中文
Your puzzle progress stays on your device.
Version 0.1.6 adds optional InMobi test ads for adults. Ads are not requested for players under 18 or players whose age is unknown. Core puzzles remain playable offline.

This policy covers Cozy Cat Sort (猫咪收纳铺), package com.kedge.cozycatsort, by individual developer kedge. Versions up to 0.1.5 contain no advertising SDK. Version 0.1.6 is an advertising integration test build with production advertising disabled. The following advertising section applies to that test build.

Local game information

Completed levels, stars and decorations, selected cat, current puzzle, undo history, language and sound/vibration/animation preferences are stored locally. The game uses the system language unless you select one manually. Version 0.1.6 also stores an age-range choice, rewarded-hint credits, ad reward receipts and local usage limits. These game records are not uploaded to a kedge server. There are no player accounts, cloud saves, chat, public profiles, user uploads, standalone analytics SDKs or in-app purchases.

Age screening and children

The game has a mixed audience. The test build offers an age-range screen without requesting a date of birth. Only the adult branch (18 or older) can access optional test ads. Choosing an age under 18, declining to answer or leaving the choice unset prevents the game from initializing the InMobi SDK or making advertising requests. Free undo, restart, the existing free hint and the existing extra basket remain available according to the game rules. Age ranges and exact ages are not sent as demographic values to InMobi; adult eligibility controls access and the SDK age-restriction setting. If a child contacts support, a parent or guardian may ask us to review or delete that correspondence.

Optional InMobi test advertising

An adult can choose to load a rewarded test ad after the free hint is used. Before loading, the game displays an explanation and a cancel option. The SDK is not initialized for advertising at app launch. Loading an ad requires an internet connection. InMobi and its delivery partners may process an IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device/operating-system and app information, network details, SDK/app-generated identifiers, technical diagnostics and ad interaction information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. This information can be processed on their servers, outside your device and country. Game progress, support emails, contacts, precise location, date of birth and exact age are not attached to advertising requests by our game.

The test build removes the Android Advertising ID permission and does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone-state, photo or storage access. Removing Advertising ID access does not mean that the SDK processes no other information. We do not provide demographic fields, precise location or a publisher-created user identifier. See InMobi's privacy policy and its links for retention, processing, partners and privacy rights. Kedge receives publisher-side delivery/revenue reporting; there is no developer-hosted player profile database.

InMobi placements are configured for global test ads in this build, and production requests are gated off in the app. Tapping “Load test ad” is not treated as consent to personalized advertising: the integration does not assert GDPR advertising consent to the SDK. A production release would require the applicable regional consent management and updated disclosures. You can decline a test ad and continue playing. Once initialized for a chosen ad, SDK operations may continue for that app session; restarting and choosing no ad avoids initializing it in the next session.

Permissions and external links

The Android test build uses internet and network-state access for optional ads and vibration for optional feedback. The privacy-policy link opens your browser, and the support link opens your email app. The game does not attach your saved progress to these links. Those services process information under their own policies.

Support, storage and deletion

If you email kedgeyuan@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them for support and retain them only as needed for the request or applicable obligations. You can request review or deletion of support correspondence at that address. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

You can delete local game records by clearing app storage or uninstalling; this may permanently remove progress. There is no kedge cloud recovery service. Deleting local game data does not automatically delete data independently held by an advertising provider, email provider, app store or operating-system service. Use the relevant provider's privacy channels or contact us for assistance; we do not invent a fixed retention period for third-party records.

This website and policy changes

This static page is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no advertising scripts, analytics or tracking cookies placed by kedge. GitHub may process technical hosting information under its General Privacy Statement. We will update this page when the game's data practices change. Questions: kedgeyuan@gmail.com.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026年10月19日 · 个人开发者：kedge

游戏进度保存在本机。
v0.1.6向成年人提供可选的InMobi测试广告；未满18岁或年龄未知的玩家不请求广告，核心关卡仍可离线游玩。

本政策适用于Cozy Cat Sort（猫咪收纳铺），包名com.kedge.cozycatsort。v0.1.5及更早版本不含广告SDK；v0.1.6为广告集成测试版本，正式商业广告尚未启用。以下广告说明适用于该测试版本。

本地游戏信息

已完成关卡、星星与家具、陪伴猫咪、当前盘面、撤销记录、语言及音效/振动/动画设置保存在本机。游戏默认读取系统语言，也支持手动选择。v0.1.6还在本机保存年龄段选择、广告提示权益、奖励凭据及次数限制。这些游戏记录不会上传至kedge服务器。游戏没有玩家账户、云存档、聊天、公开主页、用户上传、独立分析SDK或内购。

年龄选择与儿童

游戏面向混合年龄受众。测试版本提供年龄段选择，不要求出生日期。只有选择18岁及以上的分支能够访问可选测试广告；未满18岁、不愿透露或未选择年龄时，游戏不会初始化InMobi SDK或发起广告请求。原有免费撤销、重开、提示和额外篮仍按玩法规则提供。年龄段和具体年龄不会作为人口统计字段发送给InMobi；成年资格用于控制广告入口及SDK年龄限制设置。家长或监护人可就儿童主动发送的客服信息申请查询或删除。

可选的InMobi测试广告

成年人用完免费提示后，可以主动选择加载激励测试广告；加载前会显示说明与取消选项。游戏启动时不会为广告初始化SDK。加载广告需要联网；InMobi及其广告合作方可能处理IP地址及其推断的大致位置、设备/操作系统和应用信息、网络信息、SDK或应用生成的标识符、技术诊断以及广告互动信息，用于广告交付、衡量和反作弊。这些信息可能在设备之外及其他国家的服务器处理。游戏不会在广告请求中附带游戏进度、客服邮件、通讯录、精确位置、出生日期或具体年龄。

测试版本移除了Android广告标识符权限，不申请定位、通讯录、相机、麦克风、电话状态、照片或存储访问权限。移除广告标识符访问不代表SDK不处理其他信息。游戏不提供人口统计字段、精确位置或自行创建的用户标识符。第三方数据处理、合作方、保留及权利说明见InMobi隐私政策及其链接。kedge可查看发布者侧的广告交付和收益报表，没有开发者托管的玩家画像数据库。

当前广告位设为全局测试模式，应用也关闭了正式广告请求入口。点击“加载测试广告”不被当成同意个性化广告；接入代码不会向SDK虚报已获得GDPR广告同意。正式发布前仍需完成适用地区的同意管理和相应声明。你可以取消测试广告并继续游戏；选择广告并初始化SDK后，其操作可能在本次应用会话中继续。重新启动且不选择广告时，下次会话不会初始化SDK。

权限与外部链接

Android测试版的联网与网络状态权限用于可选广告，振动权限用于可关闭的操作反馈。隐私政策通过浏览器打开，客服通过邮件应用打开；链接不附带存档。这些外部服务依据各自政策处理信息。

客服、保存和删除

主动发送邮件至kedgeyuan@gmail.com时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、内容和主动附件，仅用于支持请求并按实际需要或适用义务保留。可通过同一邮箱申请查询或删除客服往来；请避免发送不必要的个人信息。

清除应用存储或卸载可删除本地记录，也可能永久丢失进度；kedge不提供云端恢复。删除本地记录不会自动删除广告、邮件、应用商店或操作系统服务商独立持有的信息；可通过相应服务商的隐私渠道处理，或联系我们协助。我们不为第三方记录虚构固定保留期限。
