Privacy policy

English简体中文
The game runs offline.
Cozy Cat Sort does not send game progress or personal information to kedge. Your progress and preferences stay on your device.

This policy describes Cozy Cat Sort (also called 猫咪收纳铺), published by the individual developer kedge, package com.kedge.cozycatsort. It covers the offline game described below. It does not describe unrelated apps or future features that are not yet included.

Information used by the game

The game stores completed levels, decoration ownership, the selected cat, the current puzzle, undo history and settings locally on your device. It reads the device language to select a supported interface language, unless you choose a language manually. Sound, animation and vibration preferences are also stored locally.

The game has no account registration, cloud save, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or in-app purchases in this version. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos, and does not collect advertising identifiers. It does not transmit this local game information to us or sell it to others.

Permissions and external links

The Android game uses vibration permission for optional feedback, which you can disable in Settings. Opening a privacy-policy link launches your browser. Opening the support email address launches your email app. Those apps and websites handle their own network connections under their own policies; the game does not attach your saved progress to these links.

If you contact us

If you email kedgeyuan@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and retain it only as needed for support or applicable obligations. You may email us to ask about or request deletion of support correspondence. Please do not send information you do not want included in a support message.

Storage and deletion

There is no developer-hosted account or cloud-progress database to delete. You can remove local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Doing so may permanently remove your progress; kedge does not provide a cloud recovery service. Device, operating-system, app-store and email providers may process information separately under their own policies.

Children and other users

The game does not ask players to provide personal information and does not provide chat, public profiles or user-content uploads. This policy applies to all players. A parent or guardian can contact us about any personal information sent through support correspondence.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The page contains no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies placed by kedge. GitHub may process technical information necessary to host and serve the website under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Visiting this website is separate from the offline game.

Changes and contact

If the game introduces services that change these practices, we will update this policy and its effective date. For questions about this policy, contact kedge at kedgeyuan@gmail.com.

中文说明

隐私政策

生效日期：2026年10月18日 · 个人开发者：kedge

游戏离线运行。
游戏进度和偏好设置保存在你的设备上，不会由游戏发送给 kedge。

本政策适用于个人开发者 kedge 发布的 Cozy Cat Sort（猫咪收纳铺），包名为 com.kedge.cozycatsort，本政策描述当前离线游戏的数据处理方式。它不描述其他应用或尚未加入的未来功能。

游戏使用哪些信息

已完成关卡、家具、陪伴猫咪、当前盘面、撤销记录及设置均保存在本机。游戏读取设备语言来选择界面语言，也允许你手动选择。音效、动画和振动偏好同样仅保存在本机。

本版本没有账户注册、云存档、广告SDK、分析SDK或应用内购买，不申请通讯录、定位、相机、麦克风或照片权限，也不收集广告标识符。游戏不会把上述本地进度发送给我们，也不会出售这些信息。

权限与外部链接

Android游戏仅使用振动权限提供可关闭的操作反馈。打开隐私政策会调用浏览器，打开客服邮箱会调用邮件应用；这些应用或网站根据各自政策处理网络连接。游戏不会在这些链接中附带存档。

主动联系客服

如果你发送邮件至 kedgeyuan@gmail.com，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容和你主动发送的附件，仅用于处理支持请求并按支持需求或适用义务保留。你可以通过同一邮箱询问信息处理情况或申请删除客服往来。请勿在客服邮件中发送你不希望提供的信息。

保存与删除

本游戏没有开发者服务器上的账户或云端进度数据库。你可以清除应用存储或卸载游戏来删除本地数据；这可能永久丢失进度，kedge不提供云端恢复。设备、操作系统、应用商店和邮件服务商可能按各自政策独立处理信息。

儿童及其他用户

游戏不要求玩家提交个人信息，也不提供聊天、公开个人主页或用户内容上传。本政策适用于所有玩家；家长或监护人可就客服邮件中主动提供的个人信息联系我们。

隐私政策网站

本页面托管于GitHub Pages。页面不含 kedge 设置的广告、分析脚本或跟踪Cookie。GitHub可能依据其隐私声明处理提供网站托管所需的技术信息。访问本网站与离线游戏本身相互独立。

政策更新与联系

如果游戏新增影响上述处理方式的服务，我们会更新本政策和生效日期。开发者：kedge；联系邮箱：kedgeyuan@gmail.com。
